The road book

Ten routes. One kingdom.
Start at the Academy, finish at Gelephu.

Learn the ropes at Route 0, then take guests from a Thimphu hotel to an airport dash, up the Dochula storm, through Trongsa’s gorge, across a night forest and out onto the southern plains.

    The garage

    Four machines.
    Four ways to be brave.

    Start in a willing little hatchback and earn your way to the Dragon Cruiser. Upgrade engines, tyres, brakes and tanks, then repaint it your own way. Cosmetics are saved, and nothing is for sale.

    Weather & wildlife

    The mountain has moods.
    So do its residents.

    All images are screenshots of the game itself, rendered in a browser. Graphics settings range from Low to Max.

    The soundtrack

    Nine original tracks.
    The mountain sings along.

    Play online

    No install. Keyboard, gamepad and touch. Add it to your home screen or desktop from the browser menu; the shell works offline, music streams while you are online.

      Privacy

      Progress and settings stay on your device. Optional online features use a guest or account identity for private cloud saves, leaderboard times you choose to submit, and feedback. Statistics are opt-in. Feedback is sent only when you submit it, and the contact address and device details are optional. Individual feedback is private to the developer. This website sets no cookies and uses no trackers.

      Installing & saves

      Windows installers may download Microsoft WebView2 if it is missing. Browser, Windows and Android saves are separate; an optional cloud profile can carry compatible progress between them. Verify the SHA-256 shown beside each download if you like.
