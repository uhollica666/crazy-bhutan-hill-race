Crazy Bhutan Hill Race

A driving game inspired by Bhutan.

The game

About the game

From busy town roads to steep mountain passes, Crazy Bhutan Hill Race brings a playful take on driving in Bhutan. Start with a basic vehicle, complete challenges, earn rewards and work your way towards more capable vehicles.

There are ten campaign levels, a driving tutorial and plenty of surprises along the way.

Is it free?
Yes. There are no ads and nothing to buy.
Where can I play it?
In your browser, or as a Windows or Android download.
Is it finished?
Not yet. It’s a public beta, so expect some rough edges.
Found a problem?
Use Feedback in the game menu, or see Help us make it better.

Levels

Explore the routes

Each level has its own passengers, road conditions and challenges. The places are inspired by Bhutan, but this is a game rather than an exact map.

    Vehicles

    Choose your vehicle

    Four vehicles with different capacities, handling and capabilities. You can upgrade them and customise their paint and accessories.

    Weather & wildlife

    More than just driving

    Road conditions change with the weather and the time of day. Along the way you’ll meet other traffic, animals and a few fictional creatures.

    All images are screenshots from the game, taken in a browser. Graphics settings range from Low to Max.

    Play online

    Nothing to install. Plays with keyboard, gamepad or touch. You can also add it to your home screen or desktop from your browser’s menu.

    Play online Recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. WebGL is required.

    Windows

    Checking the latest release...

    Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. The installers are not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may show a warning.

    Android

    Checking the latest release...

    Android 7 or newer, 64-bit ARM phones and tablets. You’ll need to allow installs from your browser once. Keep Play Protect on.

      Privacy

      Progress and settings stay on your device. Optional online features use a guest or account identity for private cloud saves, leaderboard times you choose to submit, and feedback. Statistics are opt-in. Feedback is sent only when you submit it, and the contact address and device details are optional. Individual feedback is private to the developer. This website sets no cookies and uses no trackers.

      Installing & saves

      Windows installers may download Microsoft WebView2 if it is missing. Browser, Windows and Android saves are separate; an optional cloud profile can carry compatible progress between them. You can check the SHA-256 shown beside each download.
